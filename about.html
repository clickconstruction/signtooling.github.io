How signing works

  1. Pick the document on the Documents page and press Sign. The signing form opens in a new tab.
  2. Fill in your details and sign — with your finger on a phone, or a mouse. The form tells you which fields are required.
  3. Get your copy. When you finish, a signed copy is emailed to you.

What this site is

SignTooling is a short list of the documents Click Plumbing and Electrical asks people to sign, so nobody has to hunt for the right link. The signing itself — the form, the signature, the signed PDF — runs on DocuSeal, an e-signature service. This site stores nothing and asks for no login.

Before you sign

Read the document. If anything in it is unclear, or you are not sure it is the right one for you, call the office first.
