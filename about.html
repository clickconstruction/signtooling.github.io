About This Project

What is a contract portal?

A contract portal is a centralized digital platform that streamlines the entire contract lifecycle. Click Construction has developed an innovative solution that coordinates contract delivery across large teams, providing secure document signing, efficient processing, and comprehensive management tools. Our platform enables you to create, distribute, sign, and track contracts from any device with an easy-to-use, mobile-optimized interface.

Key Features

  • PDF form fields builder (WYSIWYG)
  • 12 field types available (Signature, Date, File, Checkbox, etc.)
  • Multiple submitters per document
  • Automated emails via SMTP
  • Automatic PDF eSignature
  • PDF signature verification
  • Mobile-optimized interface
  • API and Webhooks for integrations

Implementation Details

The Click Contract Portal provides a streamlined contract management experience. Our implementation includes:

  1. Secure document signing and verification
  2. Custom contract templates for various business needs
  3. Team coordination tools for managing contract workflows
  4. Automated notifications and status tracking
